Explainable AI (XAI)

SHAP Global Feature Importance Ranking

Mean absolute SHAP value (|SHAP|) ranking calculated via TreeExplainer on the champion Random Forest model across all 601 surveyed students.

Key SHAP Insights

1. Academic Performance & CGPA are the strongest single predictors of student burnout status, capturing cumulative academic strain.

2. Screen-to-Sleep Ratio (SSDR) and Burnout Vulnerability Index (BVI) demonstrated superior predictive strength compared to raw un-engineered survey variables.

3. Fixed sociodemographics (Gender, Age group, Degree tier) contributed negligibly (|SHAP| < 0.015), showing that burnout in Bangladeshi universities is driven by lifestyle and academic pressure rather than static demographic traits.

About the research behind this tool

Burnout Radar is built on an explanatory sequential mixed-methods study that trained ten supervised classifiers and a soft-voting ensemble on survey data from 601 university students in Bangladesh, then explained the champion Random Forest model with SHAP, and triangulated the findings against 20 in-depth qualitative interviews.

The strongest predictors weren't fixed demographic traits like age or gender — they were cumulative academic performance strain, the ratio of screen time to sleep, and digital escapism as an avoidance coping mechanism. This tool lets you weigh those same factors for yourself.

Rifat Miah & A.S.M. Shihavuddin — "Explainable Machine Learning for Student Burnout Classification and Risk Stratification: A Mixed-Methods Study with Qualitative Triangulation." Presidency University, Dhaka.
